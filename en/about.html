02 / The idea's journey

One beginning.
Paths that grow.

A narrative explaining the imagined company's identity.
The following stages describe themes without inventing historical dates.

  1. 2011The starting point in the assignment

    Technology consulting and solutions

    The idea starts with a company providing advanced software tailored to the needs of different sectors.

  2. ⌘Developing the idea

    From need to product

    Thinking through the tasks, steps and screens that make an application or platform useful.

  3. ⊞Diverse paths

    Solutions across sectors

    Communication, accessibility, property, vehicles, education, commerce and hospital management share one portfolio in the project brief.

  4. ✳A developing direction

    Intelligence serving human experience

    Exploring AI for a clearly defined need, explaining its limits and keeping human review involved.
