05 / Before the next step

Questions
and clear answers.

What comes before selecting technology?+

Understand the problem, users, goals, and constraints. These help compare options around the actual need rather than the popularity of a particular tool.

Does consulting always end with choosing one tool?+

It may result in one or more recommendations and an initial experiment plan. The reasons and assumptions behind a choice should be clear before a direction is adopted.

Are these recommendations an implementation plan for a specific institution?+

No. This is an educational service concept, without a study of a real organisation or review of its systems and data.
