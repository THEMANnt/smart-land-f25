05 / Before the next step

Questions
and clear answers.

Does digitisation mean copying every paper form as it is?+

Not necessarily. The concept begins with the purpose of each step and a simpler process, then identifies what is worth moving into a digital environment.

How can a transition begin without changing everything?+

Choose a limited, well-defined process, review the experience with its users, and establish what is needed before expanding.

Does this page include a working management system?+

No. This page explains a service concept. Images and related projects are educational models, rather than systems using a real institution's data.
