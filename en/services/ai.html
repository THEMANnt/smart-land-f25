05 / Before the next step

Questions
and clear answers.

Is AI suitable for every problem?+

No. Technology should be chosen after the task and alternatives are understood. Conventional software or a redesigned process may suit some needs better.

Can the accuracy of results be guaranteed?+

This project does not guarantee accuracy. Performance depends on the task, data, and evaluation method, so the concept calls for testing, explaining limitations, and reviewing outputs.

Does this website collect data to train a model?+

No. This static educational website does not collect training data or run an AI model from this page.
