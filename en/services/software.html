05 / Before the next step

Questions
and clear answers.

Does every project need a mobile app?+

Not necessarily. The concept begins with understanding use, then comparing a website, application, or platform against the requirements. One technology is not assumed to fit every situation.

How is the first version's scope defined?+

Identify the user's essential tasks and what is needed to complete them, then separate core requirements from additions that can be reviewed later.

Is this page offering an actual contracted service?+

No. This is an educational description of a fictional Smart Land service. Deliverables and workflows are illustrative proposals, rather than a quote or commitment to implementation.
