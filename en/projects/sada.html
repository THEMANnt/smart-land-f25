?

The starting point

The challenge

Digital learning needs more than separate files or videos. Learners need a clear path showing where to begin, what they have completed, and what they can revisit later.

✳

The approach

The solution concept

The project brief describes Sada as a distance-learning platform. This concept brings course discovery, lesson organisation, and resuming where you left off into a simple interface.

↗

The goal

Intended impact

Support access to knowledge beyond the classroom and help learners follow a more organised journey. The content and lessons shown in the designs are fictional educational examples.
